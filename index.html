A Message from Our Host

Hello! Welcome to The Cryptid Orchid: A Space For Beginner Orchid Growers! Here we address each other as 'cryptids' and we discuss about our love for growing orchids: even if we are newbies at it! The reward is worth it, after all!

Here, we aren't fully familiar with keeping this plant, so beginners are meant to feel welcome! If you are intermediate or advanced and want to stay to help, that would be appreciated! And we have a Discord server too, so we can schedule meetings, and help each other out!

If you have a pressing question, or need to tell the owner of the website something directly, please click Contact the Owner to directly email! Emails will be responded within 1-2 days.
